# Logistics & Air Freight Vocabulary

## Routing & Locations

- **Lane:** Origin–Destination pair of airports (e.g. `FRA -> ORD`).
- **Pickup:** Relevant because it determines which departure airport the cargo will be shipped from.
  - *Open question:* How do pickup locations / postal codes match to airports?
- **Carrier:** Company moving the cargo (trucks or airlines/flights).
- **Transit:** Days of transport (transit time).

---

## Pricing & Quotes

- **Tariff:** Monthly price list from an airline (price per kg for each lane) — also applies to trucks.
- **Spot Quote:** One-off price that an airline issues for a specific lane/shipment.
- **Minimum:** Price floor (minimum total charge regardless of low weight).
- **Surcharges:** Additional per-kg fees (e.g. fuel, security).

---

## Weight Pricing & Brackets

- **Chargeable Weight:** Whichever is higher:
  - actual gross weight (kg), or
  - volumetric weight (`cbm × 167`)
- **Weight Breaks:** Tiered pricing brackets based on chargeable weight.
  - *Example:* `< 45 kg = 3.96 / kg`; `45 kg – 100 kg = 3.12 / kg`
- **Anti-Weight-Break (Weight Break Optimization):** If cargo weight is close to the next bracket (e.g. `96 kg`), check whether it is cheaper to declare/book it in the next higher weight bracket (e.g. `100 kg` at the lower per-kg rate).
